# Contraption workshop

A workshop for building small machines that deliver a ball through a physical chain reaction.

## Language

**Design**: The arrangement of parts before a machine runs.
_Avoid_: Save state, world

**Run**: One experiment beginning from a design.
_Avoid_: Level attempt

**Part**: A movable component taken from the parts drawer.
_Avoid_: Item, prop

**Fixture**: A starting ball, basket or switch supplied by a challenge.
_Avoid_: Decoration

**Delivery**: A ball coming to rest inside the basket after any required switch is activated.
_Avoid_: Win zone

**Parts drawer**: The available supply of components for a challenge.
_Avoid_: Toolbox
